Privacy Policy

Coin-Op City · Last updated October 1, 2026

Coin-Op City is an interactive livestream game. Its app runs on the streamer's own computer and reads the live chat of Coin-Op City's own streams on YouTube, Twitch and Kick, so that viewers can play by typing commands. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps and why.

Google and YouTube data

The app signs in to the Coin-Op City YouTube channel with Google's read-only YouTube permission (youtube.readonly). With it, the app only:

  • finds the channel's own current live broadcast, and
  • reads that broadcast's live chat messages, Super Chats, Super Stickers and new memberships.

It never posts, edits or deletes anything, and never reads anything outside Coin-Op City's own live chat. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the streamer's computer and is never shared.

Coin-Op City's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Coin-Op City uses YouTube API Services; by playing you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What is kept about viewers who play

  • Your public channel or user ID and display name on the platform you play from.
  • Your game record: coin balance, coins dropped and won, and any coin colors or name colors you unlock.

This is kept on the streamer's computer so your balance and leaderboard spot carry over between streams. Your display name and profile picture are shown on the stream while you play, as they already are in the chat.

What is not kept

  • Chat messages are read to run the game and are not stored. Ordinary chat text is never shown on stream.
  • The message attached to a paid Super Chat, bit, sub or tip may be shown and read aloud on stream once, only if it contains no links or offensive words. The audio is temporary and is not kept.
  • No email addresses, real names, payment details or location are ever collected.

Sharing

Coin-Op City does not sell, rent or share viewers' data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing your data

To have your game record removed, email 4foodllc@gmail.com with your username and platform. You can also revoke the Coin-Op City app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
