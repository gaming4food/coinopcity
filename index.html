Coin-Op City

A 24/7 interactive coin pusher livestream, played from chat.

Coin-Op City is a neon arcade coin pusher that runs live around the clock on YouTube, Twitch and Kick. Viewers play it by typing in the stream's chat: !drop drops a coin with your name on it, and every coin you push off the edge earns you points on the leaderboard.

How it works

  • Type !drop in chat to drop a coin, left or right to aim, and !coins to see your balance.
  • Coins are points only. They can never be exchanged for money, gift cards, crypto or anything else of value.
  • Supporters who send Super Chats, bits, subs or tips get a thank-you card on stream and set off special effects on the machine.

About the app

The Coin-Op City app runs on the streamer's own computer. It connects to the channel's own YouTube account with read-only access, only to read the live chat and paid messages during Coin-Op City's own broadcasts, so viewers' commands can play the game. It never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube. See the privacy policy for what it reads and keeps.
